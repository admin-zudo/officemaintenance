About us

About Zudo Works

Zudo Works is a software company in Chennai, India. We build Zoho Creator apps, Zoho CRM setups, Deluge automations, integrations and custom software for businesses in the US, UK, Australia, New Zealand and India.

Who we are

Engineers who build on Zoho every day

We replace manual processes with automated workflows, connect systems that don't talk to each other, and build custom software when an off-the-shelf product can't do the job. The people who scope your project are the people who build it.

Our team members have worked on 20+ projects for 10+ client businesses, much of it as developers at a previous organization, with hands-on experience across 15+ core Zoho applications and 25+ other Zoho products. See the projects.

Our approach

How we work

Projects succeed when they start from how the business works, not from a predetermined technology stack.

Understand first

We learn your process, your team and your goals before recommending any technology.

Design to fit

The system fits your business, not the other way around. Every customization has a clear reason.

Build reliably

Iterative builds, testing before go-live, documentation and clean deployments.

Stay after launch

Every build includes one month of free support and onboarding, then optional monthly support.

Expertise
