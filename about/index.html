Who We Are

Technology That Works for Your Business

Zudo Works is a technology company that combines custom software development expertise with deep knowledge of the Zoho ecosystem. We help businesses replace manual processes with automated workflows, connect fragmented systems into unified platforms, and build custom solutions for challenges that off-the-shelf products cannot address.

Our team has delivered 20+ projects for 10+ clients and has hands-on experience across 20+ Zoho products, from Zoho Creator and CRM to Books, Flow and Analytics. We work remotely with businesses in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand and India.

Our Approach

How We Work

We believe technology projects succeed when they start from a clear understanding of business needs — not from a predetermined technology stack.

Understand First

We invest time in understanding your business, processes, team, and goals before recommending any technology solution.

Design Thoughtfully

We design solutions that fit your business — not the other way around. Every customization has a clear business reason.

Build Reliably

We follow proven engineering practices: iterative development, thorough testing, proper documentation, and clean deployments.

Support Continuously

We do not disappear after launch. We provide ongoing support, optimization, and training to ensure long-term success.
