      How it works

      One integration and AI layer between your apps

      Each Zoho app does one job well: CRM for customers, Books for invoices, Desk for support, Creator for custom apps. The integration layer connects them with Deluge, Zoho Flow, webhooks and APIs, so a change in one app updates the others. The AI layer, using Zoho Zia or Claude and ChatGPT through Zoho MCP, reads documents and emails, sorts requests and drafts replies, with a person approving anything important.

      Data also flows back. When a contract is signed or an invoice is paid, the status syncs back to the original record, so sales, operations and finance always see the same picture.

      Manufacturing

      Manufacturing: five problems Zoho solves

      Zoho connects orders, production, stock, quality and invoicing, so the shop floor and the office work from the same live data.

      Job status lives in spreadsheets

      The problem Orders and production stages are tracked in shared sheets, so nobody knows where an order really is.

      1. Order received: A web, email or phone order arrives.
      2. AI layer: AI reads the order email and fills in the customer, items and dates.
      3. Zoho CRM: The deal is created and marked won, which starts production.
      4. Zoho Creator: A job card is created with every production stage.
      5. Shop floor: Operators tap each stage done on a tablet.The job stage syncs back to the CRM deal, so sales sees it live.
      6. Zoho Books: On dispatch, the invoice is raised automatically.Invoice and payment status sync back to the deal.
      7. Zoho Analytics: Work in progress and late orders on one dashboard.

      Result Every order’s stage is visible live, and invoices go out the day goods ship.

      Stock runs out before anyone reorders

      The problem Raw materials run out mid-job because reorder levels live in someone’s head.

      1. Zoho Creator: Every stock movement is recorded against the job.
      2. Deluge: A scheduled check compares stock with reorder points.
      3. AI layer: AI forecasts usage from open orders and suggests quantities.
      4. Zoho Cliq: Purchasing approves the suggested order in one click.
      5. Zoho Books: The purchase order goes to the usual supplier.The expected delivery date syncs back to stock.
      6. Zoho Analytics: Stock cover and supplier lead times on one dashboard.

      Result Materials are reordered before they run out, not after a job stops.

      Bills of materials are kept in Excel

      The problem BOMs live in separate spreadsheets, so costings and material lists drift out of date.
