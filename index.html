Our approach

Zoho first. Custom code when it earns its place.

Most Zoho consultancies only configure, and most software agencies only write code. We do both, so we have no reason to push you toward either. You get the simplest solution that will still hold up in two years.

  1. Configure

    If Zoho does it out of the box, we set it up properly instead of building something new.

  2. Extend

    When built-in features stop short, we extend Zoho with Deluge, Creator and Flow.

  3. Build

    When Zoho is the wrong tool, we write custom software and integrate it with your existing systems.

How we work

How a project runs

Five stages, each with something concrete you can review before we move on.

  1. Discovery call

    We learn how the work flows today, which systems are involved and what has to change.

    You get: a short summary of what we heard and an honest view on whether we're the right fit.

  2. Scope and design

    We design the data model, automations, integrations and user roles.

    You get: a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost.

  3. Build and test

    We build in stages and test integrations and data migration before anything goes live.

    You get: regular demos of working features.

  4. Go-live and training

    We move the data, switch over and train administrators and end users.

    You get: documentation your team can maintain.

  5. Support

    One month of free support and onboarding after launch, then optional monthly support.

    You get: one team that already knows your system.

Who you'll work with
