Zoho Creator

Zoho Creator development

Zoho Creator is Zoho's low-code platform for building business applications: forms, reports, dashboards, customer portals and mobile apps, with logic written in Deluge. We design and build Creator apps for the workflows your off-the-shelf tools can't handle, and connect them to the rest of your systems.

Typical uses

What businesses build on Zoho Creator

  • Field service and job management apps
  • Order, inventory and dispatch tracking
  • Approval workflows for purchasing, expenses or quotes
  • Customer and supplier portals
  • Asset and equipment registers
  • Internal tools that replace legacy Access or Excel systems
  • Inspection, audit and checklist apps for mobile teams
  • Operational dashboards that pull data from several Zoho apps

Technology

Built on the Zoho platform, extended where needed

Creator apps run on Zoho's infrastructure, so there are no servers for you to manage. When an app needs something Creator can't do natively, we extend it with Deluge, Zoho Flow, Zoho Catalyst or a small custom service, and keep that decision explicit in the design.

How it works

From first call to a live app

Most focused Zoho CRM or Zoho Creator projects take 2 to 8 weeks, depending on custom modules, integrations and data migration. Multi-app rollouts with several integrations usually take 8 to 16 weeks. We confirm the timeline in writing after a discovery call.

  1. Discovery

    We walk through the current process and the people who use it.

  2. Design

    Data model, screens, roles and automations agreed in a written scope.

  3. Build in stages

    You see working features at regular demos, not just at the end.

  4. Migrate and launch

    Existing data is imported and checked, then users are trained.

  5. Support

    Optional monthly support for fixes and new features.
