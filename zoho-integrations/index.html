Integrations

Zoho integration services

When systems don't talk to each other, people re-key data and errors creep in. We connect Zoho apps to each other and to your other systems, choosing between Zoho Flow, Deluge and custom middleware based on volume, reliability and cost.

What an integration project includes

Which system owns which data, what triggers a sync and what happens on failure.

Rules for matching records and converting data between systems.

Testing

Run against test data and edge cases before going live.

Documentation

Typical uses

Common integration patterns

  • Zoho CRM deals to Zoho Books quotes, sales orders and invoices
  • Web forms and landing pages into Zoho CRM leads
  • E-commerce orders into Zoho CRM, Books or Inventory
  • Payment gateway events updating invoices and customer records
  • Zoho Creator apps reading and writing CRM data
  • Two-way contact sync between Zoho and another platform
  • Scheduled exports to reporting or data warehouse tools
  • Webhook listeners that react to events in external systems

Technology

Choosing the right integration method

Zoho Flow is quick for simple, low-volume connections. Deluge works well inside Zoho with moderate volumes. Custom middleware, on Zoho Catalyst or standard cloud hosting, is the right choice when you need high volume, complex transformation or full control over retries and logging. We explain the trade-offs before building.

How it works

How an integration project runs

Most focused Zoho CRM or Zoho Creator projects take 2 to 8 weeks, depending on custom modules, integrations and data migration. Multi-app rollouts with several integrations usually take 8 to 16 weeks. We confirm the timeline in writing after a discovery call.

  1. Assessment

    We map the systems, data and current manual steps.

  2. Design

    Data ownership, triggers, mapping and failure handling agreed in writing.

  3. Build and test

    Developed against test data, including edge cases and failures.

  4. Go-live and monitor

    Switched on with monitoring, then handed over with documentation.
