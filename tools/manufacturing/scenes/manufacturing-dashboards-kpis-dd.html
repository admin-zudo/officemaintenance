✓Good dashboards

  • Show the trend, not just today
  • Put a target line on every chart
  • List exceptions with an owner
  • Fix each definition in writing
  • Review in the same meeting weekly

✕Poor dashboards

  • Twenty charts on one screen
  • Numbers nobody can act on
  • Averages that hide the bad job
  • Typing data in for the dashboard
  • Changing a formula quietly
