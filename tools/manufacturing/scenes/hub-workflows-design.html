✓On the floor, do

  • One scan identifies the job, lot or machine
  • Three fields or fewer per step
  • Reasons chosen from a short list
  • Large buttons for gloved hands
  • The old sheet removed on go-live

✕On the floor, avoid

  • Typing long codes
  • Forms copied from the paper version
  • Free-text reasons nobody can count
  • A login on every scan
  • Running paper and app side by side
