Zudo Works04 · How it connects

How it connects

From a tangle of tools to one integration and AI layer

Most businesses run a knot of apps and spreadsheets wired together by hand. We put one layer in the middle, so every app plugs in once and data flows, and syncs back, on its own.

  1. 1 · UntangleWe map every app, sheet and hand-off, and decide which system owns which record.
  2. 2 · ConnectDeluge, Zoho Flow and APIs link each app to the layer once, instead of to every other app.
  3. 3 · Add AI where it is checkableZoho Zia, or Claude and ChatGPT through Zoho MCP, read documents and draft records. A person approves.
  4. 4 · Sync backStatus, invoices and payments flow back to the record that started the work, so everyone sees it live.

Questions we are often asked

How much will our project cost?
It is quoted per project. After a discovery call we send a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost. Zoho licences are billed by Zoho, in your name. Our estimator at zudoworks.com/pricing shows typical hours and timelines.
How long does it take?
We start with the process that causes the most daily pain and aim to have it in use within weeks. Other processes and integrations follow one at a time.
Do we have to replace software we already use?
No. If a system works, such as your accounting package, we keep it and integrate with it.
Who owns the system?
You do. Licences and data stay in your Zoho account, and you receive documentation your team can maintain.
Do you work with businesses outside India?
Yes. We work remotely with businesses in the US, UK, Australia, New Zealand and the Middle East, with working hours that overlap with yours.
