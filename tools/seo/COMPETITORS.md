# Competitors to research before every article

Supplied by Arunkumar V on 9 October 2026. Zudo Works is an upcoming Zoho partner, and its main niche is Zoho
for manufacturing businesses. Before choosing a topic, check what these sites have published recently and what
they rank for, and look for what they leave out. This list is a starting point: also look for competitors that
are not on it, in India and in other countries.

Use this research to choose topics and find gaps. Do not criticise or name Zoho partner competitors in articles.
Product comparisons (for example Zoho vs Odoo for a manufacturer) are fine when they are fair and fact-checked.

## Also research every time

- New Zoho releases and announcements, and how they apply to manufacturers.
- AI and MCP developments, and how each can be done inside the Zoho ecosystem.
- Software development news that affects custom builds on Zoho Creator and Catalyst.
