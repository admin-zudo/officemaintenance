## 2. Week 1 set-up (once)

1. **Google Search Console:** add a Domain property for `zudoworks.com` and verify it with the DNS TXT record in Cloudflare.
2. **Submit the sitemap:** `https://zudoworks.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. **Bing Webmaster Tools:** import the site from Search Console (one click).
4. **Cloudflare:**
   - Add the www redirect rule from section 1.
   - Turn on **Crawler Hints** (Caching > Configuration). This sends IndexNow pings to Bing and others whenever pages change.
5. **Check indexing:** in Search Console, open Pages. Fix anything listed under "Discovered" or "Crawled - currently not indexed": add internal links to it, then use URL Inspection and click "Request indexing".

## 3. Content calendar (topic clusters)

The aim is at least 2 articles a week; the playbook's 3 to 5 is better if you can keep quality high. Each new article should:

- start with a two-to-three sentence quick answer;
- link to 3 to 5 existing pages and get linked from 3 to 5;
- have its own FAQ.

Add it to `tools/insights/articles_*.py`, add its title, description and quick answer, then run the commands in section 6.

Also add the article to `tools/insights/articles_watch.py`: the facts that can go out of date, where to verify them, and the date they were checked. The build copies this into `blog/.automation-history.json`, the index that automated runs read first.

## 4. Weekly Search Console routine (30 minutes, every Monday)

Record these in a sheet every week:

- total impressions and clicks;
- average CTR;
- number of queries in positions 1 to 3, 4 to 10 and 11 to 20;
- indexed page count.

Then do the two fixes below.

**Quick win 1: striking distance (positions 8 to 20)**

In Performance > Queries, filter Position to 8 to 20 and sort by impressions. For the top 3 queries:

1. Put the exact query in the page's title and main heading, if it fits naturally.
2. Add or expand the section that answers it, and add an FAQ entry for it.
3. Add 2 or 3 internal links to that page from strong pages, using the query as the link text.
4. Update the article's "modified" date in `articles_*.py`, rebuild and push, then use URL Inspection to request indexing.

**Quick win 2: low CTR (positions 3 to 10, CTR under 3%)**

Rewrite the title and meta description:

- lead with the query;
- add a year, number or bracketed hook such as [Checklist] or [With Code];
- keep the title under 60 characters;
- make the meta description promise the answer.

Also check the top gaining and top losing pages: do more of what is gaining, and refresh what is losing.

**Refresh loop (every 4 to 6 weeks per article)**

Update facts, examples and the year, add sections for new questions seen in Search Console, add internal links, and request indexing.

## 5. Off-site actions (authority and branded search)

1. **Google Business Profile:**
   - Category "Software company", with the Anna Nagar address, hours (Mon to Fri, 9:00 to 18:00 IST), services, photos and a link to `/contact/`.
   - Use exactly the same name, address and phone as the website footer.
2. **Reviews:**
   - Ask every client for a review on Clutch or GoodFirms, plus Google once the Business Profile exists.
   - Send the review links so real quotes can be added to `/work/`.
3. **Repurpose every article:**
   - a LinkedIn post (Arunkumar's profile and the company page);
   - a YouTube Short like the intro video;
   - a short email.
   - Always link back to the article.
4. **Journalist requests:** answer Zoho, CRM and low-code questions on Qwoted, Featured or Connectively for links from high-authority sites.
5. **Link the calculator:** share `/pricing/#calculator` in Zoho communities, Reddit (r/zoho) and forums when people ask about cost. Answer the question; don't spam.
6. **Unlinked mentions:** search Google for "Zudo Works" monthly and ask sites that mention you to add a link.
7. **Directory consistency:** keep Clutch, GoodFirms, TechBehemoths, Upwork and LinkedIn descriptions consistent with the site.

## 7. Honest expectations

200,000 impressions and 50,000 clicks needs a large volume of ranking pages, top-3 positions and growing branded search. For a niche B2B service, a realistic path is steady month-on-month growth over 6 to 12 months. The biggest variables are:

- how many quality articles are published;
- how many reviews and links are earned;
- how consistently the weekly routine is followed.

Track progress in Search Console, not guesses.

## 8. Manufacturing section

`/manufacturing/` is generated separately from Insights:

```
python tools/manufacturing/mfg_gen.py   # pages, guides, case studies and their figures (fails on any layout problem)
python tools/build.py
```

- Content lives in `tools/manufacturing/content_*.py`; figures are described there with `fig(...)` and rendered to `Asset/img/manufacturing/`.
- Case studies are illustrative reference implementations. Do not add client names, quotes, logos or measured results unless they are real and approved in writing.
- App screens are concept mockups and are labelled "Illustrative interface". Never present them as Zoho or customer screenshots.
- Product facts cite the pages in `content_common.SRC`. Re-check them when Zoho changes Inventory, Creator, ERP or Books editions.
