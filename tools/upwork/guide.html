Zudo Works03 · Ways to work together

Ways to work together

Three ways to contract, all through Upwork

Every payment, file and message stays on Upwork, so you keep Upwork’s payment protection and a full record of the work. We suggest the type that suits the job in our proposal.

Fixed price

Milestones

The work is split into milestones, each with a written deliverable. You fund a milestone, we deliver it, you review and release it. The next one starts only after you approve.

Best for: new apps, CRM rollouts, migrations and integrations with a clear scope.

Hourly

Tracked time

Time is logged with the Upwork desktop app and shows in your Work Diary. You set a weekly limit, and you see each week’s hours before it is billed.

Best for: fixes, investigations and work where the scope is still unclear.

Ongoing

Support block

An hourly contract with a weekly limit, or a fixed monthly milestone for an agreed number of hours, used for changes and fixes as they come up.

Best for: keeping a live system healthy after launch.

When the scope changes

It usually does. A new request is written up as a change with its effect on time and cost, and becomes a new milestone or an hourly item only after you approve it. Nothing is added to a bill without your agreement.

Zudo Works04 · How a project runs

How a project runs

Six steps, with your sign-off between each

The same sequence for a two-week integration or a three-month rollout. Only the size of each step changes.

  1. 1

    Understand

    We ask about the process, not the software: who does what, where data is typed twice, what goes wrong. A call if you want one, or written questions.
    From you30 to 60 minutes with the person who knows the process, and a few real examples.
  2. 2

    Scope and plan

    A written scope: what will be built, what will not, the milestones, and the assumptions behind them.
    From youRead it, question it, approve it. Then fund the first milestone.
  3. 3

    Build in a safe place

    We build in a sandbox or test copy wherever Zoho allows it, never straight on your live data.
    From youAccess as listed on page 9, and quick answers to questions as they come up.
  4. 4

    Demo and adjust

    Short recorded demos or calls at the end of each milestone. Changes are made while they are cheap.
    From youTry it with your own cases and tell us what feels wrong.
  5. 5

    Test and go live

    We test each workflow against written cases, then you test with real scenarios. Go-live is planned with a way back if needed.
    From youSign-off on testing and a go-live time that suits your team.
  6. 6

    Hand over and support

    Documentation, training and one month of free support and onboarding after launch.
    From youTell us what your team runs into in the first weeks.

The one rule behind every step

Nothing goes live without your sign-off, and every milestone ends with something your team can use. If a step shows the plan was wrong, we stop and agree a new one before carrying on.

Zudo Works06 · Access, security and handover

Access, security and handover

Your system, your data, your ownership

Access and security

  • A separate user for us, with only the permissions the job needs
  • Work in a sandbox or test copy wherever Zoho allows it
  • No changes to your live system without your approval of the time and the change
  • Passwords are never sent in chat; you invite our user from your admin panel
  • Your data stays in your Zoho account; we do not copy it elsewhere
  • You remove our access when the work ends, and we confirm nothing is kept
  • We sign an NDA before seeing your data if you would like one

Ownership

  • Everything built for you is yours once paid, as Upwork’s terms set out
  • Zoho licences are bought in your name, so you own the account
  • Custom code lives in your repository, not ours
  • No lock-in: any capable developer can take over from the documentation

Quality

  • Written test cases for every workflow and integration
  • Testing with your sample data before you test
  • Your sign-off before anything goes live
  • A rollback plan for every change to a live system

Zudo Works07 · Before we start

Before we start

What we need from you in week one

Having these ready usually saves a full week. You do not need all of them on day one; we will ask for each one when it is needed.

Checklist

  • One decision-maker who can approve scope, designs and go-live
  • A process walkthrough by the person who does the work today
  • Your Zoho apps and edition (for example CRM Enterprise, Creator Professional)
  • A user for us with the permissions agreed in the scope
  • Sample data or a sandbox, so we never test on live records
  • Access to connected systems (API keys created by you, test accounts)
  • Examples of the documents, emails or reports the system must produce
  • Your deadline and anything it depends on, such as a launch or an audit

Kick-off call agenda

  1. 5 minIntroductions and who decides what
  2. 20 minWalk through the current process with real examples
  3. 10 minWhat success looks like at the end of the project
  4. 10 minAccess, sandbox and data questions
  5. 10 minMilestones, demo dates and the weekly update day
  6. 5 minOpen questions and next steps

Usually one hour. We send written notes in Upwork Messages the same day.

Information that helps us quote accurately

  • Number of users and how many records you hold
  • Other systems the work must connect to
  • Anything that has been tried before and why it did not work
  • Must-have versus nice-to-have

Your Zoho licences

Zoho bills licences to you directly; they are not part of our fees. If you are unsure which apps or edition you need, we recommend the smallest setup that does the job, and say when a higher edition is really required.
